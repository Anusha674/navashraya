SMART INDIA HACKATHON (SIH26191) PROJECT DOCUMENTATION

PROBLEM STATEMENT TITLE:
Intelligent Identification of Hazard-Based Red Zones, Carrying Capacity 
Assessment, and Immediate Relocation Needs for Vulnerable Habitations

PROJECT NAME: NAVASHRAYA
MOTTO: Safe Today, Stronger Tomorrow
TARGET ORGANIZATIONS: Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA), NDRF, KSDMA
PILOT REGION: Wayanad District, Kerala, India


1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

NAVASHRAYA is an end to end intelligent decision support platform designed to 
address SIH Problem Statement 26191. The system automates multi-hazard risk 
assessment, red-zone identification, carrying capacity calculations, and 
optimal habitation relocation routing for disaster management authorities.

By combining remote sensing data (ISRO Bhuvan, Cartosat DEM), historical 
landslide inventories (Geological Survey of India), machine learning risk 
fusion, Multi-Criteria Decision Making (AHP + TOPSIS), and Explainable AI (SHAP), 
NAVASHRAYA enables proactive, data-driven relocation of vulnerable habitations 
before disasters strike.


2. PROBLEM CONTEXT & CHALLENGES

Recent extreme rainfall events in ecologically sensitive regions like the 
Western Ghats (e.g., Chooralmala and Mundakkai in Wayanad, Kerala) have 
highlighted critical operational gaps in conventional disaster management:

1. Reactive vs. Proactive Relocation: Existing emergency responses focus heavily 
   on post-disaster rescue rather than pre disaster planned relocation.
2. Isolated Hazard Assessments: Landslide susceptibility, flood plain exposure, 
   and population vulnerability are often evaluated in silos rather than as an 
   integrated multi hazard risk index.
3. Destination Overcrowding: Relocating affected populations without carrying 
   capacity analysis risks overloading infrastructure and creating new 
   vulnerabilities at resettlement sites.
4. Model Transparency Deficit: Traditional ML models act as "black boxes", 
   making field officers hesitant to act without clear feature attributions.


3. SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & CORE MODULES

NAVASHRAYA is structured into six integrated modules:

MODULE 1: GIS Spatial Red-Zone Identification
- Extracts spatial parameters (Slope, Aspect, Elevation, Stream Proximity, Road Cut 
  Proximity, TWI) using PostGIS and GeoPandas.
- Intersects Survey of India (SOI) administrative village boundaries with GSI 
  landslide polygons and KSDMA flood plain layers.

MODULE 2: Predictive AI/ML Hazard & Risk Fusion
- Supervised Machine Learning: Trains a 100 tree Random Forest Classifier on 
  spatial conditioning factors to predict landslide susceptibility (0-100%).
- Risk Fusion Engine: Integrates landslide score and flood plain exposure % 
  into a composite Multi-Hazard Score.

MODULE 3: Carrying Capacity & Usable Land Assessment
- Computes net safe usable land area for candidate relocation sites.
- Evaluates population capacity limits against Census 2011 demographic data 
  and housing/infrastructure requirements.

MODULE 4: AHP + TOPSIS Multi-Criteria Site Ranking
- Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP): Derives objective criterion weights (Safety 60%, 
  Proximity 25%, Capacity 15%) with Consistency Ratio CR < 0.10.
- TOPSIS Optimization: Calculates relative closeness vectors (Ci*) to rank 
  safe destination villages and define relocation corridors.

MODULE 5: Explainable AI (SHAP) & Scenario Simulation
- SHAP Attributions: Computes Shapley values to explain local feature impacts 
  (e.g., Slope Angle +0.24, Rainfall +0.16) for red zone habitations.
- Extreme Monsoon Simulation: Interactive +25% extreme rainfall escalation 
  toggle to stress-test hazard zones and observe safety score changes.

MODULE 6: Executive Decision Support & Report Generator
- Interactive GIS Map: Leaflet powered visual map displaying affected source 
  villages in RED, recommended safe destinations in GREEN, and relocation 
  corridors as BLUE dashed routes.
- PDF Decision Report: Instant formal decision directive generation ready for 
  printing and official administrative validation.


4. KEY MATHEMATICAL FORMULAS (SIMPLE HUMAN-READABLE FORMAT)

1. DISASTER RISK FRAMEWORK (UNDRR Standard):
   Risk = Hazard × Exposure × Vulnerability

2. MULTI-HAZARD FUSION SCORE:
   Multi-Hazard Score = (0.50 × Landslide Score) + (0.50 × Flood Exposure %)
   - High Hazard: Score >= 30
   - Moderate Hazard: Score 20 to 29.9
   - Low Hazard: Score < 20

3. SAFE RELOCATION RANKING SCORE (AHP + TOPSIS):
   Relocation Score = (0.60 × Safety Score) + (0.25 × Proximity Score) + 15
   - Proximity Score = max(0, 100 - 5 × Distance in km)

4. DESTINATION CARRYING CAPACITY:
   Available Capacity = (Usable Safe Land Area ÷ Land Required per Person) - Existing Population


5. DATASETS & PROVENANCE REGISTRY

1. ISRO Bhuvan (NRSC / ISRO):
   - Cartosat-1 30m DEM & ALOS PALSAR Datasets for elevation, slope, and TWI.
   - 1:50,000 Land Use / Land Cover (LULC) maps & satellite flood inundation overlays.
2. Geological Survey of India (GSI):
   - National Landslide Susceptibility Mapping (NLSM) 1:50,000 scale vector inventory.
3. Survey of India (SOI):
   - Official administrative village MultiPolygon boundaries (EPSG:4326 / UTM 43N).
4. Census of India:
   - Primary Census Abstract 2011 population data for Wayanad district.
5. Kerala State Disaster Management Authority (KSDMA):
   - KSDMP state hazard maps, flood plain layers, and monsoon trigger scenario parameters.


6. TECHNICAL STACK

- Backend API: FastAPI (Python 3.12), Uvicorn, Pydantic
- Database / Spatial GIS: PostGIS (PostgreSQL), GeoPandas, PyProj, Shapely
- Machine Learning & XAI: Scikit learn (RandomForestClassifier), Joblib, SHAP
- Frontend Interface: HTML5, Vanilla CSS3 (Green & White Theme), JavaScript (ES6+)
- Mapping Engine: Leaflet.js, OpenStreetMap Carto tiles


7. SOCIAL IMPACT & OPERATIONAL VALUE

- Life-Saving Proactive Resettlement: Identifies vulnerable habitations before 
  monsoon landslides occur, allowing planned evacuation and resettlement.
- Carrying Capacity Protection: Prevents destination overcrowding and ensures 
  resettlement sites have adequate infrastructure and safe land.
- Transparent AI Governance: Empowers disaster response commanders (NDRF/KSDMA) 
  with explainable SHAP metrics for confident decision-making.
- Scalability: System design can be easily adapted to any mountain or flood-prone 
  district in India using standard ISRO Bhuvan and SOI spatial layers.
