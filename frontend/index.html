Research-Backed Decision Support Methodology

×
  1. Multi-Source GIS Data: DEM, Survey of India village boundaries, GSI Landslide shapefiles, and KSDMA flood plain layers.
  2. Predictive ML Hazard Modeling: Random Forest model trained on geo-environmental conditioning factors.
  3. Risk Aggregation Formula:
    Risk Score = Hazard Score × Exposure Factor × Vulnerability Index
  4. AHP + TOPSIS Ranking: Pairwise criteria weighting and ideal vector closeness ranking for candidate relocation sites.
  5. Carrying Capacity Matching: Net usable land calculation against housing and infrastructure requirements.
